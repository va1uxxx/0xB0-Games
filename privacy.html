🔒 Privacy Policy

> what_we_collect

Nothing. We don't have accounts, we don't use analytics, we don't track you, we don't serve ads, and we don't send your data anywhere.

> local_storage

Your browser's localStorage stores your settings (theme, tab disguise, panic key). This data stays on your device and never leaves it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

> external_games

Most games are loaded from other public websites. Those sites may have their own privacy policies. We don't control them and don't share any data with them.

> cookies

We don't set cookies. If you use the unblocker, its proxy may need cookies for the sites you visit — those are handled by the proxy service, not us.

> children

This site is designed for students. We intentionally collect no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

> changes

If we ever need to change this policy, we'll update this page. Since we don't collect data, changes would only affect localStorage usage.
